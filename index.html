Why TDS is not an extra tax:

The 1% TDS (Sec 194S) is already deducted at source by Indian crypto exchanges on your sell order and remitted to the Income Tax Dept against your PAN. It acts as an advance tax credit that reduces your final 30% tax liability during annual ITR filing. You do NOT pay 30% + 1%.
